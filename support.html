FAQ

My steps are not counting.
The quest needs the Motion & Fitness permission and a phone with a step sensor. In iOS Settings → Road to Ithaka, make sure Motion & Fitness is on; in the app, Settings → Quest Tracking shows what the pedometer is doing.
How is distance calculated?
Steps × your step length. Set it under Body Measurements: Automatic estimates it from your height, or choose an exact value.
How do friends work?
Every wanderer has a username. Share yours (Friends → Invites) or type a friend's to walk together; progress syncs while the app is online.
Can I move my quest to a new phone?
Settings → Quest Management → Save Quest copies your journey; send the text to the new device and use Load Quest there.
